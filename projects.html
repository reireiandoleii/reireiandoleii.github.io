Introduction - IRL Visual Novel

This is just a short (and rather unpolished) visual novel I developed with my group mates in completion for Introduction to Film and Interactive Media. It's an original story that was spearheaded by me, with the help of my friends that tells the story of trying to 'perfect' an imperfect classroom. It tackles concepts such as 'trauma builds who you are' and 'you can't fix what isn't broken' territories, and despite being obviously very rushed with deadline, it's still something I very much cherish!

DADAISM DOCTRINE

In the rapidly advancing world of Year 613, everyone is born with "The Blessing" - a supernatural ability that grants everyone extraordinary powers, but with one massive drawback: it will go against you when you commit a sin. Since the world is at it's advent, what is considered a 'sin' is not yet known, leading people to discover it through harsh means.

Junecia Mago, a devoutly religious girl born without the Blessing, finds herself questioning the very doctrines that uphold her society. June deems to 'watch' over the Blessing, as she uncovers the complexities of human morality, identity, and forgiveness.

For in a world where everyone is expected to be "good", what truly makes a good person?

The Haunting and the Googly Eyes!

What happens when an isekai world inhabited mostly by NPCs suddenly becomes sentient?

Following the hero's death and failure to vanquish the Demon King's reign, the world's "system" breaks, freeing every non-player character (NPC) from their predetermined roles and making them aware of their own existence. This causes mass hysteria, as no one has the will to live, let alone do anything at all, now knowing that their very purpose is null and void.

The story follows the hero's seamstress, Vollidiot, who decides to join the Demon Army out of sheer boredom. There, he meets Blume, the priestess of the hero's rival party, who's just as confused as he is.

Together, Vollidiot and Blume form a comedic alliance to make use of their newfound freedom: Vollidiot creates plushie dolls, while Blume uses her corrupted priestess magic to possess them with the souls of the damned - all to haunt the very people who have wronged them both.

Polaroid

So kiss me and smile for me,
Tell me you'll wait for me.

In the year 2030, a decade after the global "nCoV" outbreak, Yanny and Laurel have lived in complete isolation on a far-off farm, without seeing another human being. With the seasons rapidly changing, their livelihood deteriorating, and Yanny pregnant with their firstborn, their marriage begins to fall apart.

One day, the couple discovers a Polaroid camera containing photographs of a city worker, alongside coordinates suggesting that humanity may still be out there. Following a heated argument, Laurel leaves for the city, promising to make Yanny rich. Months pass, and Yanny receives nothing from him but envelopes of money.

Desperate to find her husband before giving birth, Yanny follows the coordinates, only to discover an abandoned city - and Polaroid films documenting humanity's horrifying fate: malnourished, photosynthetic beings with enormous eyes, condemned to work endlessly by the virus.

Will she ever find Laurel, or merely what remains of him?

And more stuff I'm not confident yet to showcase!
